# Twelve low-cost format storyboards

Prepared: 2026-09-13. These are production hypotheses, not pre-approved factual scripts. Every finished version keeps the seed's `do_not_use` constraints, uses original visuals or asset-level rights records, and receives a final claim/source review before publication. Sound may add texture, but the opening and factual payoff must remain understandable when muted.

## F01 · Visual puzzle → reveal — Braille

- **Opening (0–3 s):** six raised dots, no alphabet, with “¿Cuántas letras caben aquí?”.
- **Build (3–11 s):** dots animate through two or three configurations while a finger-height side view explains that braille is a writing system, not a language.
- **Reveal (11–18 s):** “Louis Braille publicó su sistema en 1829. Tenía 20 años.” End on a readable six-dot cell and an invitation to identify one shown letter.
- **Factual core:** six-dot cell; Braille encountered Barbier's system before publishing his own in 1829 at age twenty.
- **Source gate:** `content/published/2026-08-28-manana.json`; strengthen any letter-level demonstration against an authoritative braille chart before rendering.
- **Do not use:** the Napoleon “night writing” origin myth; “invented it at twelve”; “braille is a language.”
- **Adaptation:** 12–18 s 9:16 Reel/video for Facebook and Instagram via API and Android where verified; Threads video or frame sequence only after its route is verified. Silent carousel fallback uses question → examples → answer.
- **Status:** storyboard ready; tactile/letter chart verification pending.

## F02 · Myth → evidence → correction — Sócrates

- **Opening (0–4 s):** the familiar line “Solo sé que no sé nada” appears stamped “¿TEXTUAL?”.
- **Evidence (4–17 s):** an original manuscript-like page isolates *Apología* 21d; a cursor moves to the supported formulation.
- **Correction (17–28 s):** “Lo que no sé, tampoco creo saberlo.” Explain in one sentence that the distinction is not total ignorance but refusing to pretend knowledge.
- **Factual core:** the familiar maxim does not appear verbatim in the surviving Platonic text; the proposed line represents the passage in *Apología* 21d.
- **Source gate:** `content/published/2026-08-20-tarde.json`; its secondary-source trail is weaker than the other seeds, so a reputable Greek/Spanish critical edition must be recorded before publication.
- **Do not use:** quotation marks around “Solo sé que no sé nada” as a literal ancient quotation; a generated “authentic manuscript.”
- **Adaptation:** 20–28 s Reel/video plus a three-card myth/evidence/correction carousel; platform-native text/poll versions only where the exact control is verified.
- **Status:** concept ready; not publishable until the primary-text source gate is strengthened.

## F03 · Object-led micro-documentary — Hedy Lamarr and George Antheil

- **Opening (0–5 s):** close crop of patent US 2,292,387 with “¿Qué hacía una pianola en un sistema secreto?”.
- **Object (5–18 s):** diagrammatic paper roll travels across 88 labelled rows while transmitter and receiver change frequency together.
- **Meaning (18–34 s):** identify Hedy Kiesler Markey and George Antheil as equal coinventors; filed 10 June 1941, granted 11 August 1942.
- **Close (34–42 s):** the Navy did not use it during the war; later recognition does not make it “the invention of Wi‑Fi.”
- **Factual core:** patent identity, dates, coinventors, frequency-hopping purpose and 88 rows/frequencies.
- **Source gate:** `content/published/2026-09-07-extra88.json`, led by the patent itself and the National Inventors Hall of Fame.
- **Do not use:** “invented Wi‑Fi/Bluetooth”; confiscated/classified patent story; diminishing Antheil's role.
- **Adaptation:** source-led 30–42 s Reel/video; patent-detail carousel; Threads text/image thread. Use a public-domain patent rendering only after asset-level rights/provenance is logged.
- **Status:** storyboard ready; source-image rights record pending.

## F04 · Three-beat illustrated story — Maria Sibylla Merian

- **Opening (0–4 s):** “No dibujó un insecto. Dibujó una relación.”
- **Beat 1 (4–13 s):** Surinam, 1699: Merian and her daughter Dorothea enter a humid garden; on-screen label is factual, not a check-in.
- **Beat 2 (13–25 s):** egg → caterpillar → natural cocoon → adult remain tied to the food plant.
- **Beat 3 (25–36 s):** Amsterdam, 1705: sixty plates become a book, with Merian carrying the publication risk.
- **Factual core:** journey 1699–1701 with Dorothea; life stages plus food plant; sixty plates in the 1705 first edition.
- **Source gate:** `content/published/2026-09-05-extra51.json`.
- **Do not use:** solo travel, company sponsorship, 72 plates in the first edition, or sole credit for disproving spontaneous generation.
- **Adaptation:** three-scene Reel/video, three-card carousel and Story; use the repaired Merian visual as style reference, not as a species-identification plate.
- **Status:** storyboard ready; first reusable style reference exists.

## F05 · Quote in context — Séneca

- **Opening (0–4 s):** “No tenemos poco tiempo…” followed by a held pause.
- **Line (4–10 s):** “…hemos perdido mucho.” Show the perfect tense as the visual hinge.
- **Context (10–20 s):** “Séneca se lo escribió a Paulino, responsable del abasto de grano de Roma.” A measured grain ledger makes the addressee memorable without inventing a scene.
- **Close (20–24 s):** “No habla de una agenda llena, sino de una cuenta ya gastada.” Label this as interpretation, not quotation.
- **Factual core:** *De brevitate vitae* 1.3; exact Spanish line; addressee Paulino and his grain-supply office.
- **Source gate:** `content/published/2026-08-17-tarde.json`; Latin text plus Loeb context.
- **Do not use:** “es que perdemos mucho,” which changes the tense; a closed birth date for Seneca.
- **Adaptation:** 15–24 s typographic Reel/video, single quote card and Story question; Threads text control.
- **Status:** storyboard ready.

## F06 · Two-voice question/answer — Hedy Lamarr and George Antheil

- **Opening (0–5 s):** voice A: “Entonces, ¿Hedy Lamarr inventó el Wi‑Fi?”. On-screen answer immediately reads “No.”
- **Answer (5–20 s):** voice B explains that Lamarr and Antheil patented a radio frequency-hopping system for torpedo guidance.
- **Challenge (20–29 s):** voice A asks why a pianola roll appears; a simple 88-row diagram answers visually.
- **Close (29–38 s):** “Importante antecedente técnico no significa inventar cada tecnología posterior.” Mark both voices as a present-day dramatization.
- **Factual core/source gate:** `content/published/2026-09-07-extra88.json` and patent US 2,292,387.
- **Do not use:** invented dialogue attributed to either historical person, or a causal shortcut to Wi‑Fi/Bluetooth.
- **Adaptation:** narrated Reel/video with burned-in captions; captioned carousel fallback; Threads short Q&A sequence. Commercial audio must be owned or explicitly licensed per destination.
- **Status:** storyboard ready; publishable voice/music master not yet available.

## F07 · Hands/tabletop demonstration — Braille

- **Opening (0–3 s):** a six-position board and six movable counters: “Lee sin mirar la forma”.
- **Demonstration (3–17 s):** an overhead hand sets two verified characters; side lighting shows the raised-dot principle. A screen-reader-friendly caption states each pattern in words.
- **Payoff (17–25 s):** compress Barbier's larger cell to the six-dot system as a clearly labelled schematic, not a historical reenactment.
- **Factual core/source gate:** `content/published/2026-08-28-manana.json`; verify every demonstrated dot pattern and the exact Barbier comparison before shooting.
- **Do not use:** inaccessible “guess by sight alone” framing; imply sighted simulation reproduces tactile reading.
- **Adaptation:** 15–25 s native/video upload, step carousel, Story quiz with a non-interactive accessible answer in the next frame.
- **Status:** storyboard ready; physical prop and accessibility review pending.

## F08 · Timeline animation — Anna Atkins

- **Opening (0–3 s):** three dates fill the screen: 1842 → 1843 → 1844.
- **1842 (3–9 s):** John Herschel invents the cyanotype process.
- **1843 (9–18 s):** Atkins begins *Photographs of British Algae*; label “fotogramas por contacto, sin cámara”.
- **1844 (18–26 s):** Talbot begins *The Pencil of Nature*.
- **Close (26–31 s):** “La innovación de Atkins fue convertir el proceso en publicación sistemática.”
- **Factual core/source gate:** `content/published/2026-09-07-extra84.json`, with Natural History Museum, V&A, The Met and Linnean Society.
- **Do not use:** say Atkins invented cyanotype; fixed plate/copy counts; call it an art book.
- **Adaptation:** code-drawn Reel/video, timeline carousel, Story tap-through and Threads image sequence. Exact dates and labels are deterministic overlays.
- **Status:** storyboard ready.

## F09 · Seamless visual loop — Cyanotype reveal

- **Opening/payoff:** a pale botanical silhouette washes into Prussian blue, then returns through the same water ripple so the cut is invisible.
- **Text beats:** “papel sensibilizado” → “objeto en contacto” → “luz solar” → “lavado” in 8–12 seconds; final frame repeats the first.
- **Factual core/source gate:** cyanotype contact process from `content/published/2026-09-07-extra84.json`.
- **Do not use:** show a camera or negative as necessary; imply the animation is documentary footage or a surviving Atkins plate.
- **Adaptation:** 8–12 s Reel/video for all verified video routes; a four-frame process carousel where video is unavailable. Music-only version must use owned/licensed audio and is a separate treatment.
- **Status:** storyboard ready; motion and audio master pending.

## F10 · Swipeable mini-essay — Merian

- **Card 1:** “¿Por qué poner cinco momentos en una sola página?”.
- **Card 2:** egg — one-line observation.
- **Card 3:** caterpillar plus its food plant.
- **Card 4:** natural pupa/cocoon, with no unsupported species label.
- **Card 5:** adult and the whole relationship.
- **Card 6:** “1705 · sesenta láminas en la primera edición” plus sources and question.
- **Factual core/source gate:** `content/published/2026-09-05-extra51.json`.
- **Do not use:** generated cards as biological identification; first-edition count of 72; invented sponsorship.
- **Adaptation:** six-image Facebook/Instagram multi-photo or carousel via every supported route; ordered Threads image sequence if supported, otherwise an explicitly linked thread. Card order must be verified after posting.
- **Status:** storyboard ready; API carousel support unresolved in the local adapter.

## F11 · Story sequence with a question — Hedy Lamarr patent

- **Frame 1:** cropped patent object: “¿Para qué servían estas 88 filas?” with A/B/C choices.
- **Frame 2:** native poll/quiz only if the brand Story composer exposes it; otherwise the choices remain visual and accessible.
- **Frame 3:** answer: synchronized changes among 88 carrier frequencies, with equal coinventor credit and patent number.
- **Frame 4 (optional):** correction: “No: esto no equivale a haber inventado el Wi‑Fi.”
- **Factual core/source gate:** `content/published/2026-09-07-extra88.json` and US 2,292,387.
- **Do not use:** reaction bait, delayed answer, patent confiscation/classification story.
- **Adaptation:** Instagram/Facebook Stories with native interaction where actually exposed; feed carousel fallback; Threads native poll is a distinct route/format and its answer wording must permit exactly one defensible choice.
- **Status:** storyboard ready; Facebook/Instagram Story controls still require a composer probe.

## F12 · Single-image editorial + strong caption — Anna Atkins

- **Image:** original 4:5 cyanotype worktable reconstruction with an algae photogram, strong negative space and deterministic Spanish overlay.
- **Overlay:** “EL PRIMER LIBRO ILUSTRADO / CON FOTOGRAFÍAS / ERA AZUL” and “Anna Atkins · 1843”.
- **Caption payoff:** the first part preceded Talbot's *The Pencil of Nature* by a year and was botanical reference material, not conceived as an art book.
- **Factual core/source gate:** `content/published/2026-09-07-extra84.json`; full approved brief at `experiments/media-lab/briefs/LAB-F12-001.md`.
- **Do not use:** claim Atkins invented cyanotype; fixed plate/copy counts; call the image a historical plate.
- **Adaptation:** Facebook/Instagram/Threads feed image through API and Android as supported; Story crop is a related format cell, not a new independent experiment.
- **Status:** API version publicly published and verified on all three mandatory networks; distinct Merian Android match is ready for a later collision-checked window.
