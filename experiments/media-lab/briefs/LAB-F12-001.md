## Verified factual core

In October 1843 Anna Atkins presented the Royal Society with the first part of *Photographs of British Algae*. Its images were cyanotype photograms made by placing specimens in contact with sensitized paper and exposing it to sunlight. The process was invented by John Herschel in 1842. Atkins's first part predates Talbot's *The Pencil of Nature* (1844). The work was botanical reference material, not conceived as an art book.
